I work in computational science and engineering, at the intersection of applied mathematics, engineering applications, data science, and artificial intelligence. My work focuses on understanding, predicting, and designing complex systems, particularly when nonlinearity, uncertainty, and interacting mechanisms make conventional approaches insufficient. Applications include mechanical metamaterials, energy harvesting, smart structures, and mathematical epidemiology.

I hold a research faculty position at the [National Laboratory for Scientific Computing (LNCC)](https://www.gov.br/lncc/pt-br){:target="_blank"} and am Professor of Applied and Computational Mathematics at [Rio de Janeiro State University (UERJ)](https://www.uerj.br){:target="_blank"}, Brazil. In both roles, I lead research, teach, and supervise undergraduate and graduate students.

My interests extend beyond producing scientific knowledge. I am particularly interested in how mathematical thinking, computational modeling, data, and AI can support innovation, technology development, risk assessment, and strategic decision-making in academia, industry, and public policy. During the COVID-19 pandemic, I coordinated *COVID-19: Observatório Fluminense*, an independent initiative that monitored the epidemic in the state of Rio de Janeiro and across Brazil.

My broader objective is to connect rigorous science with leadership and impact: building teams and collaborations, developing people and ideas, creating [computational tools](/software/), and translating complex problems into knowledge that supports better decisions and real-world solutions.

One example of public mathematical outreach is [BirthdayFractal](https://americocunhajr.github.io/BirthdayFractal/), an interactive exploration of Newton fractals generated from calendar dates, with accessible explanations and reproducible research code.

## 🧭 Leadership

Beyond research, I am engaged in scientific leadership, editorial work, and the governance of professional societies.

I serve as Editor of [*Nonlinear Dynamics*](https://link.springer.com/journal/11071){:target="_blank"} and as Associate Editor of the [*Journal of Vibration and Acoustics*](https://asmedigitalcollection.asme.org/vibrationacoustics){:target="_blank"} (ASME) and the [*Journal of Vibration Engineering & Technologies*](https://link.springer.com/journal/42417){:target="_blank"} (Springer Nature). I previously served as an Affiliated Editor of [*PLOS ONE*](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/){:target="_blank"}.

I have been elected President of the [Brazilian Association for Computational Methods in Engineering (ABMEC)](https://www.abmec.org.br){:target="_blank"} for the 2027–2028 term, and currently serve as its Second Secretary. I am also an elected Board Member of the [International Society of Nonlinear Dynamics (NoDyS)](https://nodys.org/){:target="_blank"}, based in Rome, Italy, and its only elected representative from the Southern Hemisphere.

Previously, I served on the Council of the [Brazilian Society of Mechanical Sciences and Engineering (ABCM)](https://www.abcm.org.br){:target="_blank"}, including a term as Executive Secretary of its Committee on Nonlinear and Chaotic Phenomena. I also served as Coordinator of the Dynamics, Control and Applications Committee of the [Brazilian Society of Computational and Applied Mathematics (SBMAC)](https://www.sbmac.org.br){:target="_blank"}.
